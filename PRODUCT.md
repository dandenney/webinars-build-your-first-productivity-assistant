# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical webinar attendees evaluating OpenClaw as a practical agent. They are watching a shared screen and need to understand quickly what the agent can inspect, reason about, and act on through conversation.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate a safe, reproducible post-launch workflow through chat. Success means attendees see OpenClaw move from a plain-language request to evidence gathering, calibrated judgment, and controlled action without turning the session into a terminal workshop.

## Brand Personality

Confident, practical, transparent. The presentation should feel technically credible without becoming procedural or overstating autonomy.

## Anti-references

Avoid command-heavy workshops, dense documentation slides, generic AI spectacle, unexplained magic, and claims that hide approval or authentication boundaries.

## Design Principles

1. Demonstrate through conversation.
2. Show judgment, not plumbing.
3. Make approval boundaries visible.
4. Keep operational detail available but off the main path.
5. Prefer one memorable point per slide or step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, preserve reduced-motion support, avoid color-only meaning, and keep presentation copy readable on a shared screen.
